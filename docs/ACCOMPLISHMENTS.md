# Accomplishments

Accomplishments is the private historical counterpart to Journey. Journey records everything a student pursued, including unsuccessful applications. Accomplishments includes only explicit successful outcomes supported by the type-specific Journey workflow or a structured manual record.

## Data and provenance

- Journey-derived records reference the Journey opportunity and canonical catalog ID.
- A small identity snapshot (title, organization, type, and category) is captured when the outcome is recorded so later catalog edits do not rewrite personal history.
- Personal fields such as role, dates, description, notes, result, and user-added skills remain user-authoritative.
- Manual records have no fabricated catalog source.
- Documents remain references-only. Upload storage is deferred until UnlockED has an audited private file-storage system.

## Outcome semantics

- Internships and career programs become accomplishments when completed.
- Scholarships become accomplishments when awarded; receiving funds can later update the outcome to completed.
- Research becomes an accomplishment when the experience is completed.
- Competitions may qualify through participation, finalist, placement, win, or completion outcomes.
- Meaningful programs, fellowships, leadership experiences, certifications, and projects qualify when completed. Ordinary software and discount activation does not.
- Applied, rejected, withdrawn, declined, archived, and waitlisted states remain in Journey history and never become accomplishments.

All Journey outcomes are student-reported unless an independent verification system is added later. UnlockED does not label them verified.

## Corrections and removal

Correcting a Journey item to an unsuccessful state deactivates the derived accomplishment without deleting Journey history. Removing a Journey-derived accomplishment hides it from Accomplishments; removing a manual record leaves only a private idempotency tombstone so repeated requests cannot recreate or corrupt it. Removed records are excluded from the page, search, and account export. Canonical Journey facts are corrected from Journey, while personal detail fields are edited from Accomplishments.

## Privacy and access

The record is account-scoped, private, available on Free and Pro, included in account export, and removed through the existing account-deletion path. Analytics record only bounded feature usage and never titles, notes, descriptions, award amounts, or reflection content.

Accomplishment categories are not currently fed into For You ranking. This avoids a feedback loop that would narrow recommendations toward only what the student has already completed. A future ranking integration must preserve exploration and portfolio diversity.
