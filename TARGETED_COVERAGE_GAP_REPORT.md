# UnlockED Targeted Coverage Gap Report

Verified: August 20, 2026

## Executive Summary

This was a surgical coverage pass, not a broad catalog expansion. Thirteen unique candidates were researched against official sources. Three passed the unchanged production recommendation gate and ten were rejected or deferred. The canonical catalog grew from 6,015 to 6,018 records, while recommendation-safe inventory grew from 89 to 92.

The pass improved the economics/banking and generic four-year freshman representative portfolios from four to five recommendations. The golden-profile p25 increased from four to five, average category diversity remained 4.75, and full recommendation generation remained well below the production performance budget.

Transfer and English/publishing profiles still bottom out at four. Those deserts were preserved because the remaining researched options require unproven residency, membership, institutional nomination, age, team participation, or a future application cycle. No recommendation eligibility rule was weakened.

## Remaining Unresolved Deserts

1. **Transfer:** only one record is explicitly transfer-eligible and one is transfer-specific. The Maryland 2+2, AICPA transfer, PTK, and Tau Sigma paths cannot safely rank without residency, membership, nomination, or a current open cycle.
2. **Humanities scholarship and research:** the current safe matrix still has no humanities scholarship and only one humanities research record. Several museum and writing cycles reopen in October 2026 through January 2027.
3. **Competitions:** College Fed Challenge, Atlas contests, and similar programs remain excluded because team, age, institutional, or lifecycle proof is incomplete.
4. **Scholarship breadth:** safe scholarship inventory is 8. The strongest next-cycle candidates should be re-audited on their recorded source-watch dates rather than pre-opened.
5. **Profile facts:** residency, age, institutional nomination, honor-society membership, and team participation are not safely inferable. Opportunities requiring them must continue to fail closed.
